# Lens Code font library

Open-licence font families from https://github.com/google/fonts, converted from TrueType to WOFF2 and otherwise unchanged. Each family is under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1: its own licence is beside it, in `families/<family>/OFL.txt`, with its copyright holders. The fonts may be used, embedded in a
page and shipped with a project; they may not be sold on their own. `index.json` lists each family, its designer, its axes and its files.
